## Full description (max 4000)

Turn your NVIDIA SHIELD TV into a fast 5GHz Wi-Fi hotspot that shares its wired (Ethernet) internet.

Unlike proxy-based hotspot apps, connected devices get a normal IP address and full internet access
automatically. No proxy settings, no extra apps on your phone, laptop, console or smart device.

FEATURES
• 5GHz Wi-Fi for speed, or 2.4GHz for range
• Real routing (NAT + DHCP): everything works, including games, VPNs and streaming apps
• Starts automatically when your SHIELD turns on, and repairs itself if Wi-Fi drops
• Live status on the TV: network name, band and channel, internet source, connected devices
• Designed for the remote: simple, D-pad-friendly screen
• No root required

ONE-TIME SETUP
This app uses Android's built-in hotspot features through the SHIELD's own debugging connection, so
you need to enable it once:
1. Settings › Device Preferences › About: select "Build" 7 times
2. Settings › Device Preferences › Developer options: turn on "Network debugging"
3. Open the app, press "Start hotspot" and choose "Always allow" on the prompt

GOOD TO KNOW
• On 5GHz the network appears as "DIRECT-SH-<your name>". This is a requirement of the Wi-Fi Direct
  technology that makes 5GHz possible on SHIELD.
• Your SHIELD must be connected by Ethernet.
• Network debugging stays on in the background. Only allow it on networks you trust.

Works with NVIDIA SHIELD TV and SHIELD TV Pro (Android 11). NVIDIA and SHIELD are trademarks of
NVIDIA Corporation. This app is not affiliated with or endorsed by NVIDIA.
