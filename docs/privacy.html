Privacy Policy: 5GHz Wifi Hotspot for NVIDIA SHIELD

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This app is published by the developer dsjiffry. This policy explains what the app does with data.

Summary

The app does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs and no tracking.

What the app handles on your device

  • Hotspot settings (network name, password, band, auto-start preference) are stored only in the app's private storage on your device, so the hotspot can restart automatically. They never leave the device.
  • Debugging connection. The app connects to your device's own Android debugging service at 127.0.0.1 (the device itself) to start and manage the hotspot. It generates a key that stays on the device. Nothing is sent over the internet.
  • Status information (whether the hotspot is on, Wi-Fi channel, number of connected devices) is read on the device and shown on screen only. The app does not record or transmit which devices connect.

Network traffic

Devices that join the hotspot use your SHIELD's internet connection directly. The app does not inspect, log or modify that traffic.

Purchases

If the app offers optional tips, payments are processed entirely by Google Play under Google's Privacy Policy. The developer receives no payment details.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
